## What the work looks like

Alignerr's FAQ describes tasks ranging from designing code challenges that stress-test AI, to grading outputs against strict rubrics, to generating datasets across text, audio and more ([Alignerr](https://www.alignerr.com/), checked 20 Nov 2026). Role listings on the day we checked included Generalist, AI Training Specialist, AI Policy, Ethics & Compliance Analyst and Product Review Analyst.

## Who can apply

Alignerr's FAQ says it looks for people with the skills for the tasks they apply for: domain expertise, strong coding ability or sharp analytical thinking. Many contributors are students or degree holders, but generalists are welcome. **Clear written English is a must.** Location rules are not set out on the pages we checked, but the process page asks you to check that your location is correct and says payment setup depends on where you are.

## Onboarding steps

From the [process page](https://www.alignerr.com/process):

1. Sign up with Google and upload your resume (the profile auto-fills).
2. Browse roles and apply. Each listing shows requirements and expected pay.
3. Verify your phone by text and your identity through Persona. Some jobs add a Zara AI interview or domain assessment.
4. If approved, sign the contract and set up payment through Stripe Connect or PayPal.
5. Complete onboarding, including an introduction to the Labelbox platform and the Alignerr Discord.
