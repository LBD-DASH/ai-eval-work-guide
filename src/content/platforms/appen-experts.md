## What the work looks like

Appen Experts describes contributors evaluating, annotating, generating and refining data for AI models, matched to their field. Domains listed on the day we checked included accounting, FP&A, healthcare administration, investment management, legal, linguistics, medicine and software engineering ([Appen Experts](https://appenexperts.com/), checked 20 Nov 2026). Appen's older contributors page now redirects there.

## How joining works

1. Apply and tell Appen about your experience; it then verifies you.
2. Get matched to projects in your domain.
3. Pass the project's qualification process (most projects have one).
4. Work and track your hours.

## Published pay

Appen Experts shows "up to" rates by domain and a footnote that **pay is based on location and skills**. For example, it lists linguists at up to $95/hr. "Up to" figures are the top of a range, so we do not treat them as what a new contributor should expect.

Payouts: most earnings are stated in US dollars and paid in local currency by bank transfer, PayPal, Payoneer, Airtm and other methods.

## No fee to join

The Appen Experts FAQ states: "We will never ask you to pay anything to join our projects." If someone claiming to be Appen asks you for money, treat it as a scam. See our [getting started guide](../../get-started/#scams-and-fees).
