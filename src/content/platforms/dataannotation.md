## What the work looks like

DataAnnotation describes its general work as evaluating chatbot responses, comparing AI outputs, flagging factual errors, testing AI-generated images and writing challenging prompts. Coding projects involve reviewing AI-written code, and multilingual projects cover translation and localization ([FAQ](https://www.dataannotation.tech/faq), checked 20 Nov 2026).

## Who can apply

The FAQ sets out tracks you choose at signup:

| Track | What they ask for |
|---|---|
| General | Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience, strong English writing, critical thinking, research and fact-checking |
| Multilingual | Native fluency in another language on top of English |
| Coding | Programming experience (Python, JavaScript and others) and LeetCode-style problem solving |
| STEM | Master's/PhD in maths, physics, biology or chemistry, or a bachelor's plus 10+ years' experience |
| Professional | Licensed credentials in law, finance or medicine |

All workers need reliable internet and fluent English. The FAQ we checked does not list eligible countries, so confirm during signup.

## The Starter Assessment

Most Starter Assessments take about an hour; specialist ones can take one to two hours. **You only get one attempt.** The FAQ says reviewers look for thoroughness and accuracy rather than speed, and that you will hear back by email, usually within a few days. It also says it cannot reply individually to every applicant.

## Published pay

| Track | Starting rate (per DataAnnotation's FAQ) |
|---|---|
| General | $25-$50/hr |
| Multilingual | $25-$40/hr |
| Coding | $40-$150+/hr |
| STEM | $40-$125+/hr |
| Professional | $40-$125+/hr |

Source: [DataAnnotation FAQ](https://www.dataannotation.tech/faq), checked 20 Nov 2026. These are the platform's own published ranges. The FAQ also says work availability depends on demand for your skills and your past performance, and that low-quality work can lose you access to projects.

## Identity verification

You will be asked for photos of a valid government-issued ID and a selfie video check, handled by Persona. Turn off any VPN first; the FAQ notes that work or university networks may route traffic through one without you knowing.
