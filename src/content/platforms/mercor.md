## What the work looks like

Mercor says it connects professionals to remote contract work that advances AI systems. Its site also describes evaluating how AI performs on valuable work and building evaluations against professional standards ([Mercor](https://www.mercor.com/), checked 20 Nov 2026).

## How joining works

From the [experts page](https://www.mercor.com/experts):

1. Create a professional profile.
2. Take Mercor's AI assessments.
3. Find and apply for opportunities that match your qualifications.

## Who it suits

Most roles listed on the day we checked named a profession: cardiologist, contract attorney, engineering writer, project management specialist, finance expert and similar. If you do not have a professional specialism yet, the platforms with general tracks (such as [DataAnnotation](../dataannotation/) or [Prolific](../prolific/)) may be a better first step.
