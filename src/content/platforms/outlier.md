## What the work looks like

Outlier's FAQ says that, depending on your expertise, you may generate prompts, rank AI outputs, improve model responses or do other tasks that improve AI performance. It also warns that available projects vary and may not always match your field ([Outlier FAQ](https://outlier.ai/faq), checked 20 Nov 2026).

## Who can apply

- You need a valid ID and mobile phone from your country of residence, a current resume and a LinkedIn profile.
- Some projects need no degree; others ask for a bachelor's, master's or PhD.
- Outlier calls its screening "selective" and says it reviews each resume against the minimum requirements for a domain.
- It does not sponsor visas. If you are on a visa, it must permit work where you live.

## Onboarding steps

1. Create an account and choose your areas of expertise.
2. Complete skill screenings and identity verification (the FAQ puts general onboarding at about 30 to 90 minutes).
3. Do project-specific onboarding: read the guidelines and pass assessment tasks.
4. Start tasking.

## How pay works

Outlier pays hourly or per task. Its FAQ says rates depend on expertise, project complexity and location, and that you see the rate before starting any project. Payments go out weekly on Tuesdays through PayPal, Airtm or ACH bank transfer. **Outlier does not publish a pay range, so we do not quote one.**

You work as an independent contractor with no minimum hours, so earnings depend entirely on the projects available to you and the time you put in.
