## What the work looks like

Prolific connects researchers and AI teams with paid participants. Its participants page lists three kinds of study: behavioural research (questionnaires, decision games), **Improve AI** (evaluating machine learning models and answering questions about AI and human interaction), and product and brand research ([Prolific participants page](https://www.prolific.com/participants), checked 20 Nov 2026). Every task is opt-in and you can leave a study at any time.

## Who can join

- You must be over 18 and have a verified account.
- You must live in a supported country. Eligibility is based on where you live, not your nationality.
- The "[Who can participate](https://participant-help.prolific.com/en/articles/445007-who-can-participate-in-studies-on-prolific)" article lists countries including the US, UK, Canada, most of the EU, Australia, Japan, India and South Africa.
- **The help centre is not consistent.** The [identity verification FAQ](https://participant-help.prolific.com/en/articles/445010-verifying-your-identity-faq) shows a shorter list that does not include South Africa, India or some others. If you live in one of those countries, check during signup.
- If your country is not supported you can join a waitlist, but Prolific does not guarantee access.

## Signing up

1. Join the waitlist (Prolific says it takes about 3 minutes).
2. Accept your invite when one arrives.
3. Verify your identity (Onfido) and complete a short practice task, which Prolific says takes about 15 minutes.
4. Stay in your country of residence. Using Prolific abroad or through a VPN can put your account on hold.

| Rule | Figure |

| Absolute minimum reward rate | £6 / $8 per hour |

These are the rules for individual studies. How many studies you get depends on your profile and what researchers need, so they say nothing about weekly totals.
