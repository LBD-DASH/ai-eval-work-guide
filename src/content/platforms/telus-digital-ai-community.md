## What the work looks like

TELUS Digital (formerly TELUS International; its old AI Community address now redirects) recruits remote contributors for AI and search projects. The [Online Data Analyst, Israel](https://jobs.telusdigital.com/jobs/17271777-online-data-analyst-israel) listing we checked describes reviewing map, business and address data, researching with online maps and search engines, comparing sources, and evaluating Hebrew and English search results, across task types such as Maps, Search/Relevance and Audio.

## Who can apply

Requirements are per listing. The Israel listing asks for:

- Professional fluency in English and Hebrew
- Having lived in Israel for the last 5 consecutive years, with regional and cultural knowledge
- Willingness to take part regularly each week when work is available
- A stable internet connection and a personal computer

Search the [AI Community jobs page](https://www.telusdigital.com/careers/ai-community) for listings in your country and language.

## Qualification steps

From the AI Community page (checked 20 Nov 2026):

1. Qualification: critical thinking evaluation and role-specific assessments.
2. Compliance checks: CV validation, IP address validation (confirms your location) and a check for conflicts with other projects.
3. ID verification through a secure upload link.
4. Onboarding: acceptance form, paperwork and system setup.

The Israel listing adds that you take an open book qualification exam, with guidelines provided beforehand.

## How pay works

Pay is set per listing. The Israel listing says it is **task-based rather than hourly-paid** and gives no rate, so we do not quote one. Check each listing for its own pay terms.
